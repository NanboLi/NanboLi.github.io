This thesis studies how generative models can recover the compositional structure of visual scenes without object-level supervision. It develops MulMON for learning object representations from multiple views, latent duplicate suppression (LDS) to reduce redundant object representations, and DyMON to separate camera motion from object dynamics. Together, these methods support scene factorization and predictions at new viewpoints and times.
